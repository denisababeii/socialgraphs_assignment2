In [[human]]s, saliva is around 99% [[water]], plus [[electrolytes]], [[mucus]], [[white blood cells]], [[epithelial cells]] (from which [[DNA]] can be extracted), [[enzymes]] (such as [[lingual lipase]] and [[amylase]]), and [[antiseptic|antimicrobial]] agents (such as secretory [[IgA]], and [[lysozymes]]).<ref name=\"ReferenceA\">{{cite book |last=Nosek |first=Thomas M. |url=http://humanphysiology.tuars.com/program/section6/6ch4/s6ch4_6.htm |title=Essentials of Human Physiology, Section 6, Chapter 4 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160117182014/http://humanphysiology.tuars.com/program/section6/6ch4/s6ch4_6.htm |archive-date=2016-01-17}}</ref>\n\nThe enzymes found in saliva are essential in beginning the process of [[digestion]] of dietary [[starch]]es and [[fat]]s. These enzymes also play a role in breaking down food particles trapped within dental crevices, thus protecting teeth from bacterial decay.<ref name = DCDCM>{{cite book|last1= Fejerskov|first1= O.|last2= Kidd|first2= E.|title= Dental Caries: The Disease and Its Clinical Management|edition= 2nd|year= 2007|publisher= Wiley-Blackwell|isbn= 978-1-4051-3889-5}}</ref> Saliva also performs a lubricating function, wetting [[food]] and permitting the initiation of [[swallowing]], and protecting the [[oral mucosa]] from [[desiccation|drying out]].<ref name = SOH>{{cite book|last1= Edgar|first1= M.|last2= Dawes|first2= C.|last3= O'Mullane|first3= D.|title= Saliva and Oral Health|edition= 3 |publisher= [[British Dental Association]]|year= 2004|isbn= 978-0-904588-87-3}}</ref>\n\nSaliva has specialized purposes for a variety of animal species beyond predigestion. Certain swifts construct nests with their sticky saliva. The foundation of bird's nest soup is an aerodramus nest. Venomous saliva injected by fangs is used by cobras, vipers, and certain other members of the venom clade to hunt. Some caterpillars use modified salivary glands to store silk proteins, which they then use to make silk fiber.<ref>{{cite web|title=Insect-produced Mouth soreness is common in people with reduced saliva ([[xerostomia]]) and food (especially dry food) sticks to the inside of the mouth.<ref name=statp/>\n\n===Digestion===\nThe digestive functions of saliva include moistening food and helping to create a food [[bolus (digestion)|bolus]]. The lubricative function of saliva allows the food bolus to be passed easily from the mouth into the esophagus. Saliva contains the enzyme amylase, also called ptyalin, which is capable of breaking down starch into simpler sugars such as maltose and dextrin that can be further broken down in the small intestine.<ref name=statp/> About 30% of starch digestion takes place in the mouth cavity. Salivary glands also secrete salivary lipase to begin fat digestion. Salivary lipase plays a large role in fat digestion in newborn infants as their pancreatic lipase still needs some time to develop.<ref>{{cite book\n | last = Maton\n | first = Anthea\n | title = Human Biology and Health\n | publisher = Prentice Hall\n | date = 1993\n | isbn = 978-0-13-981176-0\n | url-access = registration\n | url = https://archive.org/details/humanbiologyheal00scho\n }}</ref>\n\n===Role in taste===\nSaliva has a role in the [[Taste|sense of taste]].<ref name=statp/> It is the liquid medium in which chemicals are carried to taste receptor cells (mostly associated with [[lingual papilla]]e).<ref name=statp/> People with little saliva may experience [[dysgeusia]] (reduced ability to taste or to experience a metallic taste), occurring as an [[adverse effect]] of some [[prescription drug]]s.<ref name=\"rad\">{{cite journal |vauthors=Rademacher WM, Aziz Y, Hielema A, Cheung KC, de Lange J, Vissink A, Rozema FR |title=Oral adverse effects of drugs: Taste disorders |journal=Oral Diseases |volume=26 |issue=1 |pages=213\u2013223 |date=January 2020 |pmid=31532870 |pmc=6988472 |doi=10.1111/odi.13199}}</ref>\n\n===Disease diagnosis===\nSaliva can be used in development of [[Non-invasive procedure|non-invasive]] [[biomarker]]s to diagnose the presence or risk of some diseases, such as dental caries and [[periodontal disease]].<ref name=\"Nonaka\"/><ref name=to/><ref name=\"zhang\">{{cite journal |vauthors=Zhang CZ, Cheng XQ, Li JY, Zhang P, Yi P, Xu X, Zhou XD |title=Saliva in the diagnosis of diseases |journal=International Journal of Oral Science |volume=8 |issue=3 |pages=133\u20137 |date=September 2016 |pmid=27585820 |pmc=5113094 |doi=10.1038/ijos.2016.38}}</ref>\n\n===Other===\n* Saliva maintains the pH of the mouth.<ref name=statp/> Saliva is supersaturated with various ions. Certain salivary proteins prevent precipitation, which would form salts. These ions act as a [[buffering agent|buffer]], keeping the acidity of the mouth within a certain range, typically pH 6.2\u20137.4. This prevents minerals in the dental hard tissues from dissolving.<ref name=statp/>\n* Saliva secretes [[carbonic anhydrase]] (gustin), which is thought to play a role in the development of taste buds.<ref>{{cite book|author=Manuel Ramos-Casals|author2=Haralampos M. Moutsopoulos|author3=John H. Stone|title=Sjogren's syndrome: Diagnosis and Therapeutics|page =522|publisher=Springer, 2011}}</ref>\n* Saliva contains [[epidermal growth factor]] (EGF), which promotes cellular proliferation, differentiation, and healing.<ref name=\"Herbst\">{{cite journal | author = Herbst RS | title = Review of epidermal growth factor receptor biology | journal = International Journal of Radiation Oncology, Biology, Physics | volume = 59 | issue = 2 Suppl | pages = 21\u20136 | year = 2004 | pmid = 15142631 | doi = 10.1016/j.ijrobp.2003.11.041 | doi-access = free }}</ref> The biological effects of salivary EGF include healing of oral and gastroesophageal ulcers, inhibition of gastric acid secretion, stimulation of DNA synthesis as well as mucosal protection from intraluminal injurious factors, such as [[gastric acid]], [[bile acid]]s, pepsin, and agents that may injure the oral cavity.<ref name=statp/><ref>{{cite journal | author = Venturi S, Venturi M | title = Iodine in evolution of salivary glands and in oral health | journal = Nutrition and Health | volume = 20 | issue = 2 | pages = 119\u2013134 | year = 2009 | pmid = 19835108 | doi = 10.1177/026010600902000204 | s2cid = 25710052 }}</ref>\n\n==Production==\nThe production of saliva is stimulated both by the [[sympathetic nervous system]] and the [[parasympathetic]].<ref>{{cite book| title= Essentials of Human Physiology| first= Thomas M. |last= Nosek| chapter=Section 6/6ch4/s6ch4_7 |chapter-url=http://humanphysiology.tuars.com/program/section6/6ch4/s6ch4_7.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160324124828/http://humanphysiology.tuars.com/program/section6/6ch4/s6ch4_7.htm|archive-date=2016-03-24}}</ref>\n\nSympathetic stimulation of saliva is to facilitate [[Respiration (physiology)|respiration]], whereas parasympathetic stimulation is to facilitate digestion.\n\nParasympathetic stimulation leads to acetylcholine (ACh) release onto the salivary acinar cells. ACh binds to [[muscarinic receptor]]s, specifically M<sub>3</sub>, and causes an increased intracellular calcium ion concentration (through the IP<sub>3</sub>/DAG second messenger system). Increased calcium causes vesicles within the cells to fuse with the apical cell membrane leading to secretion. ACh also causes the salivary gland to release [[kallikrein]], an enzyme that converts [[kininogen]] to [[lysyl-bradykinin]]. Lysyl-bradykinin acts upon blood vessels and capillaries of the salivary gland to generate [[vasodilation]] and increased [[capillary permeability]], respectively. The resulting increased blood flow to the [[acini]] allows the production of more saliva. In addition, [[Substance P]] can bind to [[Tachykinin]] [[NK1 receptor|NK-1 receptor]]s leading to increased intracellular calcium concentrations and subsequently increased saliva secretion. Lastly, both parasympathetic and sympathetic nervous stimulation can lead to [[myoepithelium]] contraction which causes the expulsion of secretions from the secretory acinus into the ducts and eventually to the oral cavity.\n\nSympathetic stimulation results in the release of [[norepinephrine]]. Norepinephrine binding to [[alpha adrenergic receptor|\u03b1-adrenergic receptor]]s will cause an increase in intracellular calcium levels leading to more fluid vs. protein secretion. If norepinephrine binds \u03b2-adrenergic receptors, it will result in more protein or enzyme secretion vs. fluid secretion. Stimulation by norepinephrine initially decreases blood flow to the salivary glands due to constriction of blood vessels but this effect is overtaken by vasodilation caused by various local vasodilators.\n\nSaliva production may also be pharmacologically stimulated by the so-called [[sialagogue]]s. It can also be suppressed by the so-called [[antisialagogue]]s.\n\n==Behavior==\n\n===Spitting===\n[[File:OakDoNotSpitOnFloor.jpg|thumb|right|225px|A building being renovated in the [[Carrollton, Louisiana|Carrollton]] section of [[New Orleans]]]]\n\n[[Spitting]] is the act of forcibly ejecting saliva or other substances from the mouth. In many parts of the world, it is considered rude and a social [[taboo]], and has sometimes been outlawed. In some countries, for example, it has been outlawed for reasons of public decency and attempting to reduce the spread of disease. These laws may not strictly enforced, but in [[Singapore]], the fine for spitting may be as high as SGD$2,000 for multiple offenses, and one can even be arrested.{{citation needed|date=January 2020}} In [[China]], expectoration is more socially acceptable (even if officially disapproved of or illegal), and [[spittoon]]s are still a common appearance in some cultures. Some animals, even humans in some cases, use spitting as an automatic defensive maneuver. [[Camel]]s are well known for doing this, though most domestic camels are trained not to.\n\nSpitting by an infected person (for example, one with [[Severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus Wounds doused with NGF healed twice as fast as untreated and unlicked wounds; therefore, saliva can help to heal wounds in some species. Three years after ''[[Survival of the Sickest (album)|Survival of the Sickest]]'', Saliva released ''[[Blood Stained Love Story]]'' in 2007, repeating ''[[Back into Your System]]''{{'}}s chart performance at No.&nbsp;19 on the Billboard 200. Its first single, \"[[Ladies and Gentlemen (Saliva song)|Ladies and Gentlemen]]\", peaked at No.&nbsp;2 on the Mainstream Rock Chart. Their best-known lineup, which lasted from 2000\u20132005, consisted of [[Josey Scott]], Wayne Swinny, Chris D'Abaldo, Dave Novotny, and Paul Crosby.\n\nThe band is known for having their songs played at sporting events, especially [[WWE]]. Guitarist Wayne Swinny said, \"a lot of our songs, whether they were intended that way or not - most of them weren't - they just lend themselves to sporting events. Shortly afterward, bassist Brad Stewart would also depart to focus on other touring and projects. Saliva then released their tenth studio record, ''10 Lives'', on October 19, 2018 via [[Megaforce Records]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.blabbermouth.net/news/saliva-to-release-10-lives-album-in-october/|title=SALIVA to Release '10 Lives' Album In October|last=Blabbermouth|date=August 27, 2018|website=BLABBERMOUTH.NET|access-date=September 3, 2019}}</ref> The album was produced by frontman [[Bobby Amaru]] and Steve Perreira in [[Jacksonville, Florida]]. In 2019, both Crosby and Stewart confirmed their return to the band.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://agitatetheairwaves.com/blog/f/paul-crosby-back-on-tour-with-saliva|title=Paul Crosby: Back on Tour with Saliva!|last=Airwaves|first=Agitate the|website=Agitate the Airwaves|language=en-US|access-date=September 3, 2019|archive-date=September 3, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190903100509/https://agitatetheairwaves.com/blog/f/paul-crosby-back-on-tour-with-saliva|url-status=dead}}</ref> Saliva would go on to tour in 2019 as a part of Puddle of Mudd's Muddfest.\n\nIn October 2019, former vocalist Josey Scott announced on ''The Morning Dump'' podcast that he planned to reunite with Saliva for a tour and a new studio album.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Childers |first1=Chad |title=Josey Scott Whets Saliva Fans' Appetites With Reunion Talk |url=https://loudwire.com/josey-scott-saliva-reunion/ |website=Loudwire |date=October 28, 2019 |access-date=December 24, 2019}}</ref> This was confirmed by Swinny in an interview with Detroit's WRIF Radio, adding that although \"there's nothing on paper,\" the reunion should take place from 2020 into 2021 to commemorate the 20th anniversary of ''Every Six Seconds''. In addition to Scott rejoining, Swinny also suggested that former guitarist Chris D'Abaldo may also be taking part.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Kennelty |first1=Greg |title=SALIVA To Reunite With Original Singer Josey Scott For New Album And Tour |url=https://metalinjection.net/latest-news/reunited/saliva-to-reunite-with-original-singer-josey-scott-for-new-album-and-tour |website=MetalInjection |date=October 30, 2019 |access-date=December 24, 2019}}</ref> In January 2021, it was announced that these plans were put on hold due to the [[COVID-19 pandemic]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.blabbermouth.net/news/salivas-reunion-with-original-singer-josey-scott-has-been-put-on-hold/|title=SALIVA's Reunion With Original Singer JOSEY SCOTT 'Has Been Put On Hold'|date=January 26, 2021|website=[[Blabbermouth.net]]|access-date=January 26, 2021}}</ref>\n\nOn April 16, 2021, the band released a music video for \"After Me\" following the 20th anniversary of their second album ''Every Six Seconds''. Also, guitarist and founder Wayne Swinney sat out touring for August, due to testing positive for [[COVID-19]], with 9th Planet guitarist Josh Kulack filling in for him.\n\n[[File:Josey Scott 2022.png|thumb|upright=1.2|Josey Scott performing with Saliva at Blue Ridge Rock Festival 2022]]\nOn May 14, 2022, Paul Crosby left Saliva for the second time. The band kept performing with Lee Hutt before Sammi Jo Bishop was announced as their permanent drummer.\n\nOn September 11, 2022, Josey Scott made his first appearance with the band as a special guest since departing in 2011, where they performed three songs together: \"Your Disease\", \"Always\", and \"Click Click Boom.\"<ref>{{Cite web |last=Trapp |first=Philip|title=Saliva Reunite With Josey Scott at Blue Ridge Rock Fest |url=https://loudwire.com/saliva-reunite-with-singer-josey-scott-at-blue-ridge-rock-festival/ |access-date=2022-09-19 |website=Loudwire |date=September 12, 2022 |language=en}}</ref>\n\nOn March 22, 2023, guitarist and founding member Wayne Swinny died from a brain hemorrhage, at the age of 59.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://blabbermouth.net/news/saliva-guitarist-wayne-swinny-reportedly-dies-after-suffering-brain-hemorrhage|title=SALIVA Guitarist WAYNE SWINNY Dies After Suffering Brain Hemorrhage|publisher=[[Blabbermouth.net]]|access-date=March 22, 2023|date=March 22, 2023}}</ref> He was the last original member who still remained with the band.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://deadline.com/2023/03/wayne-swinny-dies-cofounder-of-rock-group-saliva-was-59-1235307798/|title=Wayne Swinny Dies: Co-Founder Guitarist Of Rock Group Saliva Was 59|publisher=Deadline Hollywood|date=March 22, 2023|accessdate=March 23, 2023}}</ref>\n\nIn the weeks following Swinny's death, the band announced they would be continuing in honour of him. They completed a North American co-headlining tour with [[Drowning Pool]], with support from [[Adelitas Way]] and [[Any Given Sin]], across September and October 2023.<ref>[https://blabbermouth.net/news/saliva-and-drowning-pool-announce-september-october-2023-u-s-tour SALIVA And DROWNING POOL Announce September/October 2023 U.S. Tour - BLABBERMOUTH.NET]</ref>\n\nIn May 2023, Scott announced that he would begin touring with a new band entitled Josey Scott's Saliva, performing Saliva's songs from his era as well as his new songs.<ref>[https://blabbermouth.net/news/josey-scott-announces-tour-dates-under-josey-scotts-saliva-banner JOSEY SCOTT Announces Tour Dates Under JOSEY SCOTT'S SALIVA Banner - BLABBERMOUTH.NET]</ref><ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qhNc_ocBYrs&ab_channel=BattlelinePodcast Original frontman of Saliva, Josey Scott - Exclusive interview | Ep. 186 - YouTube]</ref>\n\nIn September 2023, the band released their 11th studio album, ''Revelation''. The album, recorded over the pandemic, is the last to feature Swinny's guitar playing.<ref>{{cite web |title=New Saliva Album \"Revelation\" Due In September |url=https://www.theprp.com/2023/07/19/news/new-saliva-album-revelation-due-in-september/ |website=The PRP|date=July 19, 2023 }}</ref>\n\n== Band members ==\n{{col-begin}}\n{{col-3}}\n;Current
